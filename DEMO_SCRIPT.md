# RippleMap demo recording script

Target length: 3 minutes

Production: https://ripplemap-three.vercel.app/

Verified public example: https://ripplemap-three.vercel.app/app?share=e6ffc465-a13f-4dc1-9185-8dd7f9320182

## Before recording

- Sign in before starting.
- Use a 1920 by 1080 recording canvas.
- Set browser zoom to 90 or 100 percent.
- Close unrelated tabs and disable notifications.
- Keep this event text ready to paste:

> A surprise interest-rate decision has triggered a broad risk-off move. Which crypto assets are repricing most strongly, and what evidence supports the move?

- Use these symbols: `BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT`.
- Keep the verified public example open in a second tab as a fallback.
- Pause briefly after each click so viewers can follow the interface.

## Scene 1: The problem

Time: 0:00 to 0:25

Screen:

- Start on the homepage.
- Hold on the main headline.
- Slowly scroll just enough to reveal the three evidence layers.
- Click **Open live workspace**.

Voiceover:

> When a major event hits, market evidence, recent reporting, and AI opinions usually live in separate places. RippleMap brings those layers together in one auditable research workspace. It helps a researcher see what moved, inspect the available evidence, and challenge every inference.

On-screen caption:

> From market event to auditable research map

## Scene 2: Build the investigation

Time: 0:25 to 0:55

Screen:

- Point briefly to the live Bitget market count.
- Paste the prepared event question.
- In Symbols, type `BTCUSDT, ETH`.
- Pause to show the live dropdown.
- Select `ETHUSDT`.
- Type `, SOL`, then select `SOLUSDT`.

Voiceover:

> This workspace is connected to Bitget's live market directory. The symbol picker searches real supported instruments as I type. For this investigation, I am comparing Bitcoin, Ether, and Solana against a reported interest-rate surprise.

On-screen caption:

> Live Bitget instruments, not seeded demo data

## Scene 3: Fetch live evidence

Time: 0:55 to 1:25

Screen:

- Click **Fetch evidence and analyze**.
- While synthesis is loading, point to the live table.
- Highlight last price, 24-hour change, bid and ask, and turnover.
- Point to the observed evidence list.

Voiceover:

> RippleMap first collects the current market observations. These prices, bid and ask quotes, 24-hour movements, turnover values, and timestamps come directly from Bitget. The product keeps this observed layer separate from interpretation. A price move can coincide with an event without proving that the event caused it.

On-screen caption:

> Observation is not causation

## Scene 4: Inspect the synthesis

Time: 1:25 to 2:05

Screen:

- Wait for the synthesis to finish.
- Scroll through the summary.
- Pause on a transmission path and confidence level.
- Show the evidence and invalidation condition.
- Show contradictions, watch conditions, and caveats.

Voiceover:

> The synthesis does not simply predict a direction. It proposes a conditional transmission path, labels its confidence, identifies the evidence being used, and provides an invalidation test. It also exposes contradictions, watch conditions, and caveats. In this run, no matching news leads were available, so RippleMap explicitly lowers confidence instead of inventing confirmation.

On-screen caption:

> Confidence, contradictions, and invalidation are visible

## Scene 5: Save and share

Time: 2:05 to 2:35

Screen:

- Click **Save investigation**.
- Wait for the saved and synced message.
- Click **Create share link**.
- Open **Saved investigations**.
- Point to the `CLOUD` label.
- Open the newest saved investigation if time allows.

Voiceover:

> Signed-in researchers can save an investigation to their private cloud library and reopen it from another device. They can also create a public, read-only link for a teammate, client, or decision-maker. The underlying research remains attributable to its live evidence snapshot.

On-screen caption:

> Private cloud library plus read-only sharing

## Scene 6: Provenance and close

Time: 2:35 to 3:00

Screen:

- Open **Data sources**.
- Pause on the four source cards.
- Return to the homepage for the final sentence.

Voiceover:

> RippleMap makes the role of every layer clear: live Bitget market data, attributed reporting leads, and server-side research synthesis. It turns an event into a research map that can be inspected, challenged, saved, and shared. RippleMap is live today.

On-screen caption:

> Inspect it. Challenge it. Save it. Share it.

## Recording notes

- Speak at roughly 130 words per minute.
- Let the interface breathe. Do not move the pointer continuously.
- If synthesis takes longer than expected, explain the live evidence table while it loads.
- If a source search returns no articles, treat that as a strength: RippleMap reports missing evidence rather than fabricating support.
- Do not claim the event caused the price movement.
- Do not read exact prices aloud because they change in real time.
- End on the homepage or data-source view, not on a loading state.
